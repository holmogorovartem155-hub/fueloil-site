About

FuelOil was born in Moscow as a full band. Today, we are a duo — Yaroslav and Artem. Sometimes loss opens new doors.

Inspired by early 2000s emotional hardcore, chaotic punk and dark alternative rock, the band combines raw guitars, desperate vocals and honest lyrics.

Every song tells a story about fear, memories, loss, hope and surviving another night.

We're currently searching for a guitarist and a bassist/keys player who feel the same fire and the same pain.

"The world doesn't get better. We just become louder."

"We're not trying to be heroes. We're just writing what hurts."
